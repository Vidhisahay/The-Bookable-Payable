# Design notes

## What I learned about the documents

A PDF is an evidence bundle, not necessarily one invoice. It can contain a payable, continuation pages, remittance information, or supporting documents. Repeated invoice numbers and tax figures in attachments do not create additional obligations. The invoice gross, a cash amount after withholding, and a tax-purpose currency conversion are different values. The ERP total is a useful arithmetic check, but matching it does not prove the extracted line and tax structure is faithful.

## What the system does with unfamiliar evidence

`ingestion.pipeline.IngestionPipeline` extracts native PDF text when it is usable and otherwise applies local Tesseract OCR page by page with bounded retries. Successful OCR is cached atomically by PDF content, page, OCR settings, engine/model identity, and processing version. Timeout and empty results are not cached. The classifier uses document-text cues and keeps page-level evidence and uncertainty. `ingestion.payables.extractor.extract_payables` extracts candidate fields and retains page references and field statuses; it does not fill absent amounts to make totals balance. `ingestion.autodraft_pipeline.AutodraftPipeline` scopes invoice pages, resolves only supported master-data matches, maps candidates to the required keys, and keeps diagnostics outside the accounting JSON.

`ingestion.autodraft.reconcile` calls the supplied `erp_book` with the extracted components. The pipeline emits a payable only when the arithmetic passes, supplier identity is supported by a source-evidenced name or VAT ID attributable to the issuer, and schema enums are valid. A failed or incomplete case remains in diagnostics. Uncertain documents stay unresolved rather than being forced into `declined`. This is a deterministic extraction and validation pipeline, not a runtime LLM agent or accounting-repair loop. Local Tesseract supplies OCR; no paid service is required. The run guide documents the command and output folders.

The extractor now accepts unnumbered rows when a table header explicitly identifies description, quantity, unit price, tax, and amount columns. It keeps row tax rates at line scope and removes a matching header tax summary only when the printed line bases/rates reproduce the printed tax total. Page evidence also keeps normalized word boxes from native PDF text and, when multiple party cues appear in scanned OCR, Tesseract TSV boxes with confidence. Nearby lines and PDF/OCR blocks remain available separately from flat text. Explicit supplier, buyer, ship-to, and payee labels take priority; a narrow issuer cue can also be used when a unique legal entity shares the visual baseline to the right of an explicit Invoice Date label. Page position alone never selects the supplier. Competing spatial roles or VAT IDs stay unresolved. The new layout evidence identifies the seller block in INV-14 and INV-15, but both documents remain unbookable because the “Business Rates” line type is not established by the schema evidence.

For scanned pages whose OCR exposes an `Activity / Tax / Qty / Rate / Amount` table, ingestion keeps a separate, cached 220-DPI table-oriented OCR result. For this layout, line prices are marked net only when printed line extensions sum exactly to the printed subtotal and the printed header tax plus subtotal equals the printed gross. Itemized transport rows remain line items rather than being duplicated as header freight charges.

## A document that remains unresolved

`INV-31.pdf` is still unresolved. The native text contains `$17,657.53`, but `$` alone does not identify USD, CAD, SGD, or another dollar currency. The remittance and billing pages also contain date clues that do not establish one invoice date, and the current extractor does not recover supported electricity line components from the billing layout. Without those components and a currency, a schema-valid draft cannot be checked meaningfully against the ERP. I have left the payable un-emitted rather than choosing a currency or reconstructing lines from the gross.

`INV-14.pdf` and `INV-15.pdf` have source-supported invoice numbers, dates, amounts, and table rows, and their extracted tax rates remain on the individual lines. They are not emitted because `Business Rates` and `Utilities` do not establish an allowed line item type. Spatial party evidence resolves the seller, but arithmetic agreement cannot supply a missing schema category.

`INV-01.pdf` illustrates a different limit: its dates, EUR 438 gross, two lines, and zero-rate reverse-charge context support an ERP arithmetic result of EUR 438. The legal name in the upper-left addressee block cannot safely be assigned as seller. A bare VAT ID after the payable total is not attributed to the issuer either. The system retains that ID as ambiguous evidence in diagnostics and withholds the payable; arithmetic agreement alone cannot establish supplier identity.

## Current run and trade-offs

The latest full run used a 60-second OCR budget and one retry. It produced 42 accounting JSON files and 42 diagnostics files: two payables (INV-27 and credit memo DU-10), one confirmed decline (DU-08, a payment reminder), and 39 unresolved documents. There were no timeouts; INV-23 pages 2 and 3 yielded no usable OCR text. The other 109 pages yielded 99 OCR results and 10 native-text results. Of 33 extracted candidates, six passed arithmetic reconciliation and 27 remained unresolved. Four arithmetic-valid candidates were withheld: INV-01 for unattributed supplier identity, and INV-02, INV-14, and INV-15 for unestablished line types. INV-36 is withheld: its source prints a quantity and extended amount but no unit price, so copying the extension into `unit_price` would invent its role. INV-27 reconciles to KES 70,654.30 from seven printed activity amounts totalling KES 60,908.88 plus header VAT of KES 9,745.42; its unlabelled reference and ambiguous date remain blank. DU-10's two goods lines carry 20% VAT at line scope. Its supplier name and VAT ID come from the seller's legal footer; a different VAT ID in the customer block is excluded. Both emitted payables pass the unchanged ERP's gross check, which does not prove every source interpretation. Cached pages were reused.

The standard dataset command is documented in `RUN_GUIDE.md`; diagnostics record OCR settings and per-attempt timing. Once a page is cached, reruns reuse successful text; failed, blank, and sparse results are retried only within a fixed budget. There is no accounting revision loop: validation terminates after one ERP calculation per candidate. A future loop would need independently source-supported alternative interpretations and a strict stopping budget; an ERP delta alone cannot justify changing amounts. Current limits include incomplete spatial table reconstruction, missing evidence on some pages, and ambiguous line types. Validation remains strict so that coverage does not depend on guessed suppliers, currencies, line types, or tax treatment.
